# Chess App how and where to play:
Written by Vien Pham, complete with UI and AI engine.
Currently, the main AI algorithm is complete, but it is not too strong. Will lose to those with high Chess ELO ratings (1700+).


How to play:
The game is hosted on Vercel, so you can just follow the link(s) below:

https://vpchess-an66ypd0g-computerviruswannabes-projects.vercel.app/

If that doesnt work, try this one:

https://vpchessts.vercel.app/

Rules:
- If you play against AI, your pieces are always the ones at the bottom of the board.
- Click on a piece to see where it can move. I have displayed possible move squares with yellow highlights.
- Sometimes we don't pay attention to the last move that our opponent made. Therefore, I have highlighted the last move in light yellow to help solve this problem.
- A king in check will have the square it is standing on turn green, and it will flash red. The game is over when your king has no more legitimate squares to move to.
- Rules like castling, pawn promotion, and en passant are all accounted for.
- Choose to play either against AI or another person. Enjoy!

  Known bugs:
- The checkmate notification pops up too early when the AI wins.
